# Privacy Policy for Document Scanner

**Effective date:** 21 October 2026
**App name:** Document Scanner
**Package name:** com.sabid.documentscanner
**Developer:** [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY NAME]
**Contact:** [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]

Document Scanner ("the app", "we", "us") is a document scanner that works entirely on your device. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: **we do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information.**

## 1. Summary

- No account or sign-up is needed.
- Your scans, text, and settings stay on your device.
- There are no ads, analytics, trackers, or crash-reporting tools.
- Scanning, PDF creation, and text recognition (OCR) all happen on your device.
- Nothing is uploaded to us or to any server we operate. We do not run any servers for this app.

## 2. Information we collect

**We collect no personal information.** We do not receive your name, email address, location, device identifiers, advertising ID, contacts, usage data, or any of your documents.

## 3. Information that stays on your device

The following is created and stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot access it.

- Scanned and imported pages (photos, images, and PDFs)
- Text recognised from your documents
- Document titles, folders, tags, and favourites
- Your app settings and preferences
- If you turn on app lock: a salted, hashed version of your PIN, kept in your device's secure storage (Android Keystore-backed storage or iOS Keychain). The PIN itself is never stored in readable form.

## 4. Permissions the app asks for

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| **Camera** | To photograph the documents you choose to scan. The camera runs only while the scanner screen is open. Images are processed on your device and are not sent anywhere. |
| **Photos / media (system picker)** | To import existing images or PDFs. You choose each file, and the app only receives the files you select. |
| **Biometrics (fingerprint / face unlock)** | Optional. Used only to unlock the app if you enable app lock. Your device's operating system performs the check. The app never receives or stores your biometric data. |
| **Storage (older Android versions only)** | Optional. Used only to save an exported file to your Documents folder when you choose to. |
| **Internet** | Used only if you choose to download an optional text-recognition language pack (see section 5). The app works fully offline without it. |

You can turn off any permission at any time in your device settings. Some features will then stop working (for example, you will not be able to scan with the camera).

## 5. Internet use

The app works offline. It connects to the internet only when you ask it to download an additional text-recognition language. That request fetches a public language data file from a content delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net). The request contains none of your documents, text, or personal information. As with any web request, the network provider may receive your IP address; we do not receive or log it.

## 6. Sharing, exports, and backups

Your files leave the app only when **you** choose to export, share, or back them up.

- **Export and share:** When you share a PDF or image, it goes to the app or service you pick. That app or service handles it under its own privacy policy.
- **Backups:** The backup feature creates an encrypted file (AES-256-GCM) protected by a password only you know. We cannot recover the password or open the backup. If you lose the password, the backup cannot be restored.

We do not sell your data, share it with third parties for advertising, or use it for profiling.

## 7. Third-party services

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services. Text recognition uses open-source software (Tesseract) that runs entirely on your device.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

All data is under your control on your device.

- Delete individual documents in the app. They stay in Trash for the retention period you set, then are permanently removed.
- Empty Trash at any time.
- Use **Settings › Privacy › Delete all data** to erase everything the app has stored.
- Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## 9. Children's privacy

The app may be used by people of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). The app has no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases, no chat or social features, and no way to send information to us.

Parents and guardians should know that scans, photos, and files a child creates stay on the device. A child can share them with other apps only by using the share or export feature. If you have questions or concerns about children's privacy, please contact us using the details below.

## 10. Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Optional app lock (PIN and biometrics), hiding the app's contents in the app switcher, and encrypted backups help protect your documents. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we also recommend keeping your device locked and up to date.

## 11. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold any personal information about you, there is no personal data on our side to access, correct, export, or delete. You can see and delete everything the app stores directly within the app.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and change the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
