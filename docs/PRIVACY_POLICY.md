# Privacy Policy — Document Scanner

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

Document Scanner ("the app", package `com.sabid.documentscanner`) is designed to work entirely on your device.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting. We do not receive, store, sell or share any personal data.

## Your documents
Scanned and imported pages, recognised text, folder names, tags and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Image processing, PDF creation and text recognition (OCR) all run on your device.

## Camera and photos
The camera is used only while the scanner screen is open, to capture the documents you choose to scan. Photos are accessed only through the system picker, and only the photos you select are shared with the app.

## Internet use
The app works offline. It connects to the internet only if you choose to download an optional text-recognition language; that request downloads a public language file and contains none of your documents or text.

## Sharing, exports and backups
Files leave the app only when you export, share or create a backup. Copies you send to other apps or services are governed by those apps' policies. Backups are encrypted with a password only you know; it cannot be recovered.

## App lock and biometrics
If you enable app lock, a salted hash of your PIN is stored in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore-backed storage). Biometric checks are performed by your device's operating system; the app never receives biometric data.

## Deleting data
Delete individual documents (they stay in Trash for your chosen retention period), empty Trash, or use Settings › Privacy › Delete all data. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
